02 / NUTRIENTS

Read food
like a system.

Nutrients interact. Preparation changes availability. The body’s needs change with age, health and life stage. Explore three lenses without reducing food to a single score.

Breastfeeding matters

FROM 6 MONTHS

Add diverse foods

Complementary foods add nutrients and eating experiences while breastfeeding continues.

09 / SOURCES & METHOD

Follow the evidence.
Keep the context.

This companion summarizes and visualizes the book’s arguments. It does not reproduce the book and does not replace original research, current law or professional medical advice.

01

Attribution

Survey findings, study estimates, official rules, reported allegations and the author’s arguments use different labels.

02

Comparability

NFHS-5, CNNS and administrative measurements use different samples, age groups and years. They are not one time series.

03

Medical caution

Nutrient requirements and health conditions vary. The site offers literacy, not diagnoses, supplement doses or personalized diets.
